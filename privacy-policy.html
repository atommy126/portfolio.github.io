Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, and disclosed in connection with:


  • This website;
  • Any games, software, or applications published under the name "atommy";

Throughout this Privacy Policy, "atommy", "I", and "my" refer to Thomas Anderson, the owner and operator of this website and related software projects.


By using this website or any software published by atommy, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.


Information Collected
Website Information

When you visit this website, certain technical information may be collected automatically by the hosting provider or other infrastructure services. This may include:


  • IP address;
  • Browser type and version;
  • Device information;
  • Operating system;
  • Referring website;
  • Date and time of access.

This information may be collected by hosting providers and infrastructure services for website operation, security, maintenance, and abuse prevention.


This website does not currently use advertising networks or behavioral tracking technologies.


Game Information

Games published by atommy may collect diagnostic information when errors or crashes occur. This information may include:


  • Game version;
  • Operating system version;
  • Error messages;
  • Exception reports;
  • Stack traces;
  • Other technical information necessary to diagnose software issues.

This information is used solely to identify, investigate, and resolve bugs and technical problems.


Information Not Intentionally Collected

atommy does not intentionally collect:


  • Names;
  • Email addresses (except when voluntarily provided through support communications);
  • Payment information;
  • Steam credentials;
  • Passwords;
  • Personal documents;
  • User-generated content unrelated to crash diagnostics.

Crash Reporting

Games published by atommy may provide an optional setting allowing users to enable or disable automatic crash reporting. Users may enable or disable automatic crash reporting at first launch and through the game's settings.


Games published by atommy may also create crash log files on the user's device for diagnostic purposes regardless of crash report settings.


When crash reporting is enabled, games may generate or transmit crash reports to help improve software stability and reliability. Crash reports are transmitted only when crash reporting has been enabled by the user. When disabled, crash reports will not be transmitted through crash reporting services.


Crash reports may contain technical diagnostic information, including:


  • Game version;
  • Operating system version;
  • Error messages;
  • Exception reports;
  • Stack traces;
  • Other technical information necessary to diagnose software issues.

Crash reports are used exclusively for debugging, maintenance, and software improvement purposes.


If third-party crash reporting services are used, crash report information may be processed by those providers in accordance with their own privacy policies.
